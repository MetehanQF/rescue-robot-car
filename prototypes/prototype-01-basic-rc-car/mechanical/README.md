# Mechanical Assembly Notes - Prototype 01

## Chassis Structure

The first prototype used a two-level chassis structure.

The lower level was used for:
- DC motors
- Motor drivers
- Battery pack
- Power distribution

The upper level was used for:
- STM32F4 Discovery board
- ESP32 communication module
- Sensor connections
- Wiring and control electronics

## Observations

- The structure worked for basic movement tests.
- The design was not modular enough.
- Accessing electronic components was difficult after assembly.
- Cable routing needed improvement.
- Maintenance and debugging were not practical.

## Problems

- Motor wiring and signal wiring were too close to each other.
- The chassis did not provide enough space for future sensors.
- Battery placement affected weight distribution.
- Replacing components required partial disassembly.
