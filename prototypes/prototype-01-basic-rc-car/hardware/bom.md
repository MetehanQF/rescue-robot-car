# Bill of Materials - Prototype 01

| Component | Quantity | Purpose |
| STM32F4 Discovery | 1 | Main motor control unit |
| ESP32 DevKit V1 | 2 | Wireless controller and vehicle communication |
| BTS7960B Motor Driver | 2 | DC motor driving |
| DC Motor | 2 | Rear wheel drive |
| 3S2P 18650 Battery Pack | 1 | Main power source |
| BMS Board | 1 | Battery protection |
| LM2596 Buck Converter | 1 | Voltage regulation from battery to 5V |
| HC-SR04 Ultrasonic Sensor | 1 | Distance measurement |
| XT60 Connector | 1 | Main battery connector |
| Wires and Connectors | Various | Power and signal connections |
| 3D Printed Chassis Parts | Various | Mechanical structure |

## Notes

The first prototype was mainly designed to test movement, communication and power distribution.  
The mechanical design was functional but not modular enough for easy maintenance.
